🎲 RNGesus Take the Wheel

No steering. No sensible little guardrails. The Census rolls the whole character record, including preset classes, NPC classes, custom classes, and the stranger corners of the backstory.

Anything mechanically legal is fair game. The occasional bizarre result is intentional. Vvardenfell has floating wizards, sentient trees, and people who think the moon is a god. A questionable career choice is not where we draw the line. RNGesus also ignores ordinary naming conventions: the name may combine a documented given name from any race or gender with a documented family name or title from any race.
One roll creates the complete record.
